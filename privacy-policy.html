Privacy Policy

Effective date: April 12, 2026  ·  Last updated: April 12, 2026

PropEasy ("we", "our", or "us") is a property management application built for independent landlords. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding your data.

By using PropEasy, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

1. Information We Collect

Account information

  • Email address and password (used to create and authenticate your account)
  • Full name (optional, used for display and greeting)
  • Profile photo (optional, stored in our cloud storage)

Property and tenant data you enter

  • Property addresses and nicknames
  • Tenant names, contact details (email, phone), lease dates, and rent amounts
  • Rent payment records
  • Expense records including amounts, categories, vendors, and notes
  • Maintenance request descriptions and photos submitted by tenants

Device and usage information

  • Push notification token (to send rent reminders and lease expiry alerts)
  • Device type and operating system version (collected by Expo for crash reporting)

Payment information

Subscription payments are processed by Stripe. PropEasy never sees or stores your credit card number. Stripe retains payment data under its own privacy policy.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and operate the PropEasy service
  • To authenticate your account and keep your data secure
  • To send push notifications about rent due dates and expiring leases (only if you grant notification permission)
  • To process subscription payments through Stripe
  • To improve the app based on aggregate, anonymized usage patterns

We do not sell your personal information to third parties. Ever.

3. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored on Supabase, a hosted PostgreSQL database platform with row-level security enabled. All data is encrypted at rest and in transit over HTTPS/TLS.

Profile photos are stored in Supabase Storage with access controls ensuring only you can manage your own photo.

We apply reasonable technical and organizational safeguards, but no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. We encourage you to use a strong, unique password.

4. Third-Party Services

PropEasy uses the following third-party services:

  • Supabase — database, authentication, and file storage. Privacy policy →
  • Stripe — subscription billing and payment processing. Privacy policy →
  • Expo / EAS — app build infrastructure and push notification delivery. Privacy policy →

5. Tenant-Submitted Data

PropEasy allows landlords to share a web link with tenants so tenants can submit maintenance requests. When a tenant submits a request, we collect the information they provide (name, description, photo). This data is stored in the landlord's account and is visible only to that landlord.

Tenants do not create PropEasy accounts and are not tracked beyond the information they voluntarily submit through the maintenance request form.

6. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, your data is permanently removed from our systems within 30 days, except where we are required by law to retain certain records.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

  • Access a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data ("right to be forgotten")
  • Object to or restrict processing of your data
  • Export your data in a portable format

To exercise any of these rights, email us at support@propeasy1.netlify.app. We will respond within 30 days.

8. Children's Privacy

PropEasy is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
