Frequently Asked Questions

How do I add a tenant to a property?

Tap the Tenants tab, then tap the + button in the top right. Fill in the tenant's name, contact details, unit information, and lease dates, then tap Save. The tenant will appear under their property immediately.

How does the maintenance request link work?

Each property has a unique submission link. On the Tenants screen, tap Share Link next to a tenant's name to send them the link. When they open it, they can submit a maintenance request with a description and photo. You'll see it in the Maintenance tab instantly.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Go to Settings and tap Manage Subscription. This opens the Stripe billing portal where you can cancel, update your payment method, or view past invoices. Your access continues until the end of the current billing period.

Is my data backed up?

Yes. All your data is stored securely in the cloud using Supabase, a hosted PostgreSQL database. It is not stored only on your device — you can log in from a new phone and access all your properties, tenants, and records immediately.
